toy sentences:
   the nurse checks the chart .
   the nurse greets the patient .
   the doctor reads the chart .

'the' is followed by a word 6 times:
  nurse    2 of 6  ->  P(nurse | the) = 0.333
  chart    2 of 6  ->  P(chart | the) = 0.333
  patient  1 of 6  ->  P(patient | the) = 0.167
  doctor   1 of 6  ->  P(doctor | the) = 0.167

the chain rule, one probability per word (the sentence starts after a full stop):
  P(the | .) = 1.000
  P(nurse | the) = 0.333
  P(checks | nurse) = 0.500
  P(the | checks) = 1.000
  P(chart | the) = 0.333
  P(. | chart) = 1.000
product of the six numbers = 0.0556   (1/18 = 0.0556)
average loss per word = 0.4817   perplexity = e^loss = 1.619
